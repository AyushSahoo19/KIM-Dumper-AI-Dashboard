How to read this lab

Provenance: raw August 8 CSV (45-col telemetry) → binned aggregates. Thresholds from OEM & mine practice.

  • Heatmap: High fuel (>90 L/h actual) at low speed (<5 km/h) = idling waste / injector dribble. High fuel at 15-20 km/h on flat = overload or road drag.
  • RPM Dumping: HD785-7 dumping RPM spec 650–1900; sustained >1950 during hoist = over-rev / operator throttle abuse; <600 = lug / stall risk.
  • Gradient-Retarder: On grades < -5° retarder must be active >60% of time; <30% = foot-brake riding → overheating & fade.
